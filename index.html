Biography

I am a Ph.D. student (2023-2028) jointly at the College of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, Fudan University, and the Shanghai Innovation Institute, advised by Prof. Xipeng Qiu and Prof. Xuanjing Huang. I received my bachelor’s degree with honors from Northeastern University (2019-2023).

My current research focuses on AI Scientist, reinforcement learning, and multimodal reasoning.

I am always open to academic collaboration and discussion. Please feel free to contact me at jqtong25@m.fudan.edu.cn.
